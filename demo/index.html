What this is and isn't. This is not a copy of a fly's mind, and flies do not play Snake. The network's connections come from a real brain wiring map (FlyWire FAFB v783): a subcircuit of visual projection and descending neurons, with synapse counts turned into fixed weights and inhibition assumed from predicted neurotransmitters. Only a small interface (157 numbers) was trained. It maps simple game sensors (where the food is relative to the head, and how close walls or the body are in each direction) onto input neurons, and reads the action from output neurons. The network shown is a typical one: the median of 10 training seeds, scoring 26.7 food per game on 500 test games. Across 10 seeds the real wiring scored 27.3 ± 3.6 food per game and a degree-preserving shuffled graph 26.9 ± 2.4. A network with no internal connections at all scored 28.9 ± 2.9. The real fly wiring was no better than random wiring at this task.
